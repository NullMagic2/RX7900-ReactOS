Linux PCI core references (GPL-2.0-only), 2026-10-22

src/common/pci_caps.cpp and src/reactos/pci_config.cpp reimplement, in authored
C++17, algorithms of the Linux v6.12 PCI core: capability-list walks with TTL
(__pci_find_next_cap_ttl, pci_find_next_ext_capability), pci_cfg_space_size_ext,
pcie_capability_reg_implemented, pcie_get_speed_cap/pcie_get_width_cap,
pcie_bandwidth_available, the Resizable BAR helpers and 1002:731F quirk,
pci_save_state/pci_restore_state ordering and pci_resize_resource preconditions.
The Linux files are GPL-2.0-only; no source text is copied, but the behaviour is
derived from them, so their provenance is recorded here. drivers/pci/rom.c and
arch/x86/pci/fixup.c were consulted for the ROM BAR and VGA shadow behaviour
implemented in src/reactos/vbios.cpp. Pinned copies: work/upstream-gpuinit.
