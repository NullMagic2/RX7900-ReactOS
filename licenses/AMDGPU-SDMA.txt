Adapted source: Linux v6.12 drivers/gpu/drm/amd/amdgpu/sdma_v6_0.c and
drivers/gpu/drm/amd/amdgpu/sdma_v6_0_0_pkt_open.h.
IH decoding additionally adapts drivers/gpu/drm/amd/amdgpu/amdgpu_ih.c,
drivers/gpu/drm/amd/include/soc15_ih_clientid.h and
drivers/gpu/drm/amd/include/ivsrcid/gfx/irqsrcs_gfx_11_0_0.h.
GFX11 system leaf PTE preparation adapts the layout and constants from
drivers/gpu/drm/amd/amdgpu/gmc_v11_0.c, amdgpu_vm.h and
drivers/gpu/drm/amd/include/navi10_enum.h. Prepared entries are not installed
GPU mappings; native hierarchy publication and TLB confirmation are required.
The narrow GFXHUB3.0 VMID0 register protocol adapts gfxhub_v3_0.c,
gmc_v11_0.c, gfx_v11_0.c, amdgpu_gmc.c and amdgpu_ttm.c. Discovery validation
adapts amdgpu_discovery.c and include/discovery.h; register offsets/masks use
include/asic_reg/gc/gc_11_0_0_offset.h and gc_11_0_0_sh_mask.h.
Native physical acquisition and full MC/PSP/GFX power/cache setup are separate;
the protocol fails if its checked prerequisites are absent.
The adaptation preserves packet order, opcodes, UC fence memory type, GCR cache
rinse, byte ring pointers, ring padding and the Linux 4 MiB transfer policy.
Linux allocation APIs, lock primitives and hardware initialization are not
copied. See THIRD_PARTY.txt for pinned source hashes.
