Module: rx7900.licenses.amdgpu_firmware
Purpose: Preserve AMD firmware container attribution and adaptation boundaries.
Created: 2026-10-21
Architecture: firmware.cpp validates metadata; psp_boot.cpp executes a narrow
native-owned PSP13.0.0 bootloader protocol with retained staging ownership.
vram_discovery/nbio/ucode/psp_ring/smu13/asic_init implement physical Navi31
ASIC initialization protocols; all were tested only against synthetic devices.

Linux baseline: v6.12, downloaded unmodified on 2026-10-21.
